PIPACE — FIX RIGID-BODY BOUNCE / UNSTABLE CONTACTS
====================================================
Read AGENTS.md, rigid/RigidBodyEngine.cpp, rigid/RigidBodyTypes.h,
fluid/FluidEngine.cpp (dynamic occupancy / displace / moving boundaries),
and main.cpp worldTick() before changing anything.

This is a CORRECTNESS / STABILITY pass on the existing pixel-native rigid
bodies. It is not a feature pass. Do not add cut/merge/fracture, gases, SACE,
or a new fluid solver. Do not replace pixel masks with Box2D rectangles.
Do not retune water constants. Do not “fix” bounce by silently deleting
volume or by teleporting bodies to rest.

Observed problem:
Drawn / spawned rigid bodies will not settle. They chatter, hop, tumble, and
look generally broken on dry ground and in water.

1. WHAT ALREADY EXISTS (keep this architecture)
======================================================================

- Authoritative local-space MaterialId pixel mask per RigidBody
- Continuous pose (x, y, theta) + vx, vy, omega
- Mass / COM / inertia from occupied mask cells and material density
- Inverse rasterization onto the world grid (occupiesWorldCell)
- Dynamic solid occupancy in FluidEngine (dynamicSolid + surface velocity)
- Topology-safe fluid displacement from occupied cells
- Pressure gathered as force/torque onto wetted rigid surface cells
- Draw tool 4, materials wood/stone, Shift+F1–F10 scenes, --rigid-benchmark
- worldTick order today:
      rigid.step(fluid, PHYSICS_DT)
      fluid.simulationTick()
      rigid.gatherFluidForces(fluid)

Keep the mask authoritative. Keep fluid conservation. Keep existing test
scenes. Compile and run after each meaningful change.


======================================================================
2. ROOT CAUSES — FIX THESE, DO NOT JUST LOWER CONSTANTS
======================================================================

A. FAKE PENETRATION DEPTH (primary bounce cause)
------------------------------------------------
collectStaticContacts() only fires when a rigid body OVERLAPS a static solid
cell, then writes a constant:

    c.penetration = 0.65f;   // static
    c.penetration = 0.35f;   // body-body

Actual overlap is never measured. Positional solve then does:

    corr = max(penetration - 0.05, 0) * 0.55   // ≈ 0.33 cells EVERY contact

There is no slop based on real depth, and the value never shrinks as the
body separates.

B. POSITIONAL CORRECTIONS STACK PER OVERLAPPING PIXEL
-----------------------------------------------------
A 4-wide block resting on a floor can overlap 4 floor cells. Each contact
independently translates the whole body by ~0.33 cells. 4 contacts × 3
positional iterations ≈ several cells of upward launch per substep.

This alone explains perpetual hopping on flat ground.

C. CONTACTS ARE GENERATED INSIDE THE OBSTACLE, NOT ON THE SURFACE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Static contacts are: “world cell is static solid AND occupiesWorldCell”.
Contact point is the solid cell center (x+0.5, y+0.5), i.e. inside the wall.
Normal is the sum of directions toward non-solid neighbors, with a COM-delta
fallback.

Consequences:
- No contact until the body has already tunneled into the wall
- After the shove-out, overlap disappears → no contact → gravity pulls back
  in → overlap → shove. Classic discrete chatter loop
- Corner / edge solids produce sideways or diagonal normals
- Lever arm rA is too long (point is inside the floor) → spurious torque,
  tumbling on landing

D. CONSERVATIVE 5-TAP RASTERIZATION CREATES EXTRA OVERLAPS
----------------------------------------------------------
occupiesWorldCell() samples center + 4 inset corners. A body that is only
grazing a floor cell still occupies it, which creates a full fake-0.65
contact. Occupancy also flickers on/off for grazing cells as pose jitters.

E. VELOCITY SOLVE AND POSITION SOLVE FIGHT
------------------------------------------
Per substep: 8 sequential velocity impulses (restitution 0.04) then 3
positional translations. Sleeping bodies skip velocity impulses but still
receive positional correction, so “asleep” bodies get pushed out of their
support and then fall back in.

solveContacts() also forces a.sleeping = false on any applied impulse.

F. NO REAL MANIFOLD / NO CCD
----------------------------
- Body-body: last-writer-wins occupant grid; same-cell overlap is ignored
- Total contacts capped at 96; static contacts capped at 48 per body
- Fast falls (gravity 39.24 cells/s², clamp 70 cells/s) can skip thin floors
- No swept test between previous pose and new pose

G. SPLIT RIGID/FLUID TICK + UNBOUNDED PRESSURE KICK (water bounce)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Rigid completes ALL of its substeps and then syncOccupancy() teleports the
solid mask into the fluid and dump-displaces volume. Fluid then solves.
gatherFluidForces() applies raw neighbor pressure * fill as fx/fy/torque
with no clamp, no dt-aware scaling, and a one-frame delay.

A body slamming a pool produces a huge divergence/pressure spike, which
launches it next tick. Wood (density 0.45) is especially violent.

H. SMALLER BUGS THAT MAKE IT FEEL “SUPER BUGGY”
-----------------------------------------------
- Positional correction has no angular component, while velocity impulses do
- bodySupported() uses a different test than the contact manifold
- Painted strokes do not merge with existing bodies
- Thin rotated features can hole or over-occupy (5-tap vs 1-pixel bars)
- Damping 0.9985/tick is too weak to hide the energy the solver injects
- applyMovingBoundaryVelocity() is correct in spirit, but coupling is not
  substep-shared with the rigid integrator


======================================================================
3. REQUIRED FIX DIRECTION
======================================================================

Change the collision model. Do not keep constant penetration and hope a
smaller kRestitution hides it.

3.1 Contact generation (static world)
- Generate contacts from rigid SURFACE occupancy, not from overlapped
  interior wall cells.
- For each world cell occupied by the body, if an orthogonal neighbor is
  static solid (or world-solid), emit at most one contact on that FACE.
- Contact point = face center (the interface), not the solid cell center.
- Contact normal = out of the solid, into the body (for y-down floor:
  normal (0, -1) pushing the body toward smaller y).
- Penetration = how far that body cell (or sampled local mask) sits past
  the solid face, in cells. Measure it. Clamp to a small max (e.g. 1 cell)
  for stability, but NEVER use a constant 0.65.
- Deduplicate / merge contacts that share the same body + the same solid
  face-normal cluster so a long flat floor does not create N independent
  full-body translations.
- Prefer: weight positional correction by 1/N, or accumulate a single
  normal/tangent manifold per body-vs-supporting-plane, or use an
  accumulated-impulse solver with one effective constraint per contact
  that does not re-apply the full COM translation N times.

3.2 Positional correction
- slop ≈ 0.02–0.08 cells
- Baumgarte / correction fraction small (≈ 0.2–0.4) and based on REAL depth
- Apply at the contact point (linear + angular) OR apply linear only after
  merging contacts so net translation ≈ the max (not the sum) of depths
- Skip positional correction for sleeping, fully supported bodies whose
  max real penetration is inside slop
- Do not inject velocity during positional correction

3.3 Velocity solve
- Sequential impulses are OK if they use the updated velocity and a real
  normal
- Restitution 0 while vn is below a threshold that includes ordinary
  landing speeds after gravity at 30 Hz; v1 may use 0 restitution until
  bodies actually rest, then optionally add a tiny bounce later
- Friction at the face, Coulomb clamped against the normal impulse
- Warm-start or persist contacts across substeps if easy; not required
- Do not wake a sleeping body for impulses with |vn| below sleep band
- After the solve, if supported and |v| / |omega| below sleep thresholds
  for kSleepTicks, zero velocities and sleep

3.4 Rasterization vs collision
- Keep inverse rasterization from the source mask (do not bake rotated
  pixels back into the mask)
- Use a stable occupancy rule for fluid exclusion (conservative is OK so
  water cannot leak through)
- Collision may use a slightly stricter test (center sample, or coverage
  ≥ 0.5) so grazing floor cells do not become contacts
- If those two grids differ, document it; do not let collision run on
  conservative-only overlaps with fake depth

3.5 Body-body
- Detect overlap via occupant neighbors AND same-cell conflicts
- Same-cell: produce a contact with normal from COM delta or from the
  previous separating axis; penetration from overlap
- Remove the hard 96 cap or replace it with a per-pair cap that cannot
  drop floor contacts

3.6 Sleep / support
- Support = has a contact with normal opposing gravity (ny < 0 in y-down)
  whose real penetration is finite, OR a static solid immediately below a
  surface cell
- Do not treat “occupant below” from a stale grid as the only support test
- Sleeping bodies must not be positionally shoved every substep

3.7 Fluid coupling (stability first, not a new coupling architecture)
Do NOT implement the full shared scheduler from the roadmap in this pass
unless a tiny per-substep exchange is clearly necessary.

Minimum water-stability changes:
- gatherFluidForces only on surface occupant cells (already roughly true)
- Clamp total pressure acceleration per body this tick (e.g. a few g)
  and/or use a low-pass / hydrostatic-dominant force:
      F += -p_neighbor * n, then clamp |F|/mass and |torque|
- Zero or strongly attenuate pressure force when the neighbor fill is a
  one-tick displacement spike
- After rigid.step, displacement must remain conservative and
  connectivity-aware (already true — keep it)
- Optional small linear drag vs surrounding fluid velocity so float/sink
  scenes decay instead of oscillating
- Do not apply last frame’s unrebuilt pressure to a body that just
  teleported into a new cell cluster without clamping

Leave a comment that a future pass should share substeps:
  rigid integrate → rasterize/displace/moving BC → fluid project → forces
  inside the same substep. Do not do that full rewrite unless dry contacts
  are already stable and water still launches bodies.

3.8 Integration
- Keep gravity 39.24 cells/s² and PHYSICS_DT = 1/30
- Keep CFL-ish rigid substeps, but substeps must collide against the
  *updated* pose with real contacts; increasing substeps without fixing
  fake penetration will not stop the bounce
- Light damping is fine; it is not the fix

5. ACCEPTANCE TESTS
======================================================================

Use existing Shift+F1–F10 scenes plus a drawn stroke. Pause with Space and
single-step with S while debugging.

Must pass:

1. Shift+F1 falling stone block on the painted floor
   - Lands, maybe one short compression, then STAYS PUT
   - Sleeps (debug overlay COM turns sleep color; G view darkens)
   - No perpetual hop, spin, or sideways walk

2. Shift+F2 irregular stone
   - Settles on a stable orientation or a clearly damped rock, then sleeps
   - Must not keep kicking itself off the floor

3. Shift+F4 rotation / wall
   - Can rotate and collide the wall without exploding
   - Comes to rest if it would physically rest

4. Two drawn bodies dropped onto each other
   - Stack or tumble, then rest
   - Must not jitter-launch

5. Shift+F6 wood in pool (float) and Shift+F7 stone in pool (sink)
   - May splash and bob a few times
   - Must decay toward floating/sinking, not fly out of the pool
   - Volume error / lostR must not explode; displacement remains
     connectivity-aware

6. Shift+F8 cup
   - Body itself must not bounce-lock on the floor
   - Water containment can stay imperfect; do not special-case cups

7. Thin geometry (Shift+F9)
   - Must not disintegrate from rasterization holes
   - One-pixel bars may look stairstepped while rotating; that is OK
   - Must not vibrate to pieces on the floor

8. --rigid-benchmark still runs and writes the TSV
   - Timing can be a bit higher if contacts are more correct
   - Conservation numbers must not get worse in an obvious way

Debug while fixing:
- L overlay: contacts should sit on surfaces, normals pointing out of
  walls, not from deep inside floor pixels
- G view: occupancy should match the drawn mask at identity pose
- After rest, lastContacts should be a small stable floor set, not a
  flickering cloud
